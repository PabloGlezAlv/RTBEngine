Privacy Policy

RTBEngine is a personal portfolio game engine and prototype project operated by Pablo Gonzalva. This policy explains what information may be processed when RTBEngine is used for development, testing, or multiplayer experiments.

Information We May Process

RTBEngine does not run its own public account database. When Epic account login or Epic Online Services features are used, Epic may provide limited account information needed for authentication and multiplayer testing.

  • Epic account identifiers, product user identifiers, and display names.
  • Authentication state needed to sign in and connect to Epic Online Services.
  • Lobby, session, peer-to-peer, and presence data needed for multiplayer tests.
  • Diagnostic logs, error messages, and local configuration data generated by the application.

How Information Is Used

  • To authenticate users during development and testing.
  • To test online features such as lobbies, peer-to-peer networking, and account-based login.
  • To debug crashes, login failures, networking issues, and engine behavior.
  • To improve the project as part of a personal portfolio.

Epic Online Services

RTBEngine integrates Epic Online Services (EOS) as a third-party SDK solely to enable authentication and online multiplayer features during development and testing. RTBEngine uses EOS to request authentication tokens and to access lobby, session, and peer-to-peer networking functionality. RTBEngine does not operate its own Epic account infrastructure and does not store Epic account credentials.

Data Sharing

RTBEngine does not sell personal data. RTBEngine transmits only the minimum data required by the EOS SDK to authenticate and connect users during development and testing sessions. Diagnostic information may be reviewed by the project developer to fix bugs and validate online features.

Data Storage And Retention

RTBEngine may store local configuration files and logs on the user's device. The project does not currently maintain a separate hosted backend database for player accounts. Local files can be removed by uninstalling the project build or deleting its local data folder.

Children

RTBEngine is a development and portfolio project. It is not directed to children under 13, and it is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions about RTBEngine, contact the project developer at pablogonzalva2001@gmail.com.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated as the project changes. The latest version will be published on this website.
